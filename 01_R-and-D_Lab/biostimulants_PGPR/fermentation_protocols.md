# 🧫 Bio-fertilizers & PGPR Fermentation Protocols

Protocols for liquid culture production, microbial stabilization, and bio-input formulations using Plant Growth-Promoting Rhizobacteria (PGPR) and fungal biocontrol agents.

## Target Microorganisms & Strains
* **Bacillus spp. (PGPR):** Nitrogen fixation assistance, phosphate solubilization, and systemic resistance triggering.
* **Trichoderma spp.:** Fungal biocontrol agent and organic matter decomposition accelerator.

## Liquid Culture Media Compositions

| Formulation | Target Organism | Carbon Source | Nitrogen / Mineral Base | Target Aeration / pH |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FERM-BAC-01** | *Bacillus strains* | Cane Molasses (30 g/L) | Yeast Extract (5 g/L), $MgSO_4$ (0.5 g/L) | High Aeration | pH 7.0 |
| **FERM-TRI-01** | *Trichoderma strains* | Sucrose / Glucose (20 g/L) | Peptone (5 g/L), $KH_2PO_4$ (1 g/L) | Moderate Aeration | pH 5.5 - 6.0 |

## Fermentation Workflow
1. **Inoculum Preparation:** Re-hydrate or transfer pure strain from slant agar to 250 mL starter flask (24–48h shake culture).
2. **Bioreactor Scale-Up:** Transfer starter to liquid fermenter under continuous sterile aeration (0.2 $\mu$m air filter).
3. **Harvesting & Stabilization:** Harvest at peak stationary phase (spore/endospore formation). Add organic stabilizers prior to liquid packaging.
