# 🍄 Fungal Substrates & Formulation Matrix

Substrate compositions designed for saprophytic and functional fungal species, optimized for biological efficiency and structural water retention.

## Base Formulations

| Recipe ID | Target Species | Primary Carbon Source | Nitrogen / Mineral Supplement | Hydration Target (%) | Sterilization / Pasteurization |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SUB-PLEU-01** | *Pleurotus spp.* | Shredded Cereal Straw (80%) | Wheat Bran (15%), Gypsum (5%) | 60 – 65% | Hydrated Lime / Steam Pasteurization |

## Preparation & Condition Notes
* **Biochar Integration:** Up to 5% enriched biochar can be added to increase aeration and CEC (Cation Exchange Capacity) in bulk bags.
* **Substrate Moisture Determination:** Moisture percentage target is verified using gravimetric measurement ($M_{target} = \frac{W_{water}}{W_{dry} + W_{water}} \times 100$).
